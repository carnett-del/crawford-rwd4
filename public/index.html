An online utility portal concept for Crawford County RWD 4

This working demo shows how the district could handle meter readings, monthly billing, and customer payments in one simple system — in the office, in the field on a phone, or by customers at home.

  • Meter readings — entered by route; usage calculates automatically and unusually high use is flagged (a possible leak) before it becomes a big bill.
  • Billing — a month's bills generate in one click from the district's rate structure, with paid, due, and past-due tracking and CSV export for the bookkeeper and annual audit.
  • Customers — see their own usage history, submit a meter reading, and pay their bill online, which means fewer late accounts and fewer calls to the office.

Everything here is sample data — no real accounts, no real payments. Try it yourself: start on the Staff dashboard, then switch to the Customer Portal (top right) and pay a bill.
